Map info
========

Border lines
	Ward borders:
		stroke white 1.75mm, fill over stroke

	Ward internal borders (for rivers)
		stroke DARK .5mm, fill over stroke

	Outside the wards
		fill e6e6e6ff
		stroke bfbfbfff .5mm, fill over stroke

	Water dividing line clones:
		87cce9ff  2px dotted stroke, pattern 3 3
		dbf1f9ff  4px stroke

Stations
	Round
		15mm diameter w 1mm black stroke
		Shadow:
			15x15mm circle with 5mm DARK stroke
	
	Dept Store Hexes
		Hex 20.299 x 17.579mm (without border)
			22.608 x 19.579 with 2mm black stroke added
		Center dot: 5x5mm black circle (no stroke)
		Shadow:
			same size with 6mm DARK stroke
	
Track
	Regular track
		White stroke 1.5mm wide
		"Clone original path (LPE)"
		Add Path Effect: "Pattern Along Path"
		Paste pattern onto new cloned path
		Clone: black fill with no stroke
		Lock clone

	Permanent track between stations:
		Draw 2 lines between center points of connected stations:
		Overlay:
			2pt white stroke
		Underlay:
			4pt black stroke of connecting 2 regular stations
			5pt black if connecting a department store
	
Customer Queue card rectangles
	2.5 x 3.5" with 1.5mm black stroke
	rect radius (in SVG) is rx=22.5, ry=22.5

Map text labels:
	Ward Label text
		black text with 1mm white border
		Paris: Arial Black - Heavy - 20pt Roman Numerals, 18pt name
		Seoul: Apple SD Gothic Neo - Ultra Bold - 22pt
		Osaka: Hiragino Kaku Gothic Std - Normal - 22pt Japanese / 18pt Roman

	Station Label text
		fill text 666666ff with 1mm white border
		Paris: Arial Black - Heavy - 14pt
		Seoul: Apple SD Gothic Neo - Ultra Bold - 13pt
		Osaka: Hiragino Kaku Gothic Std - Normal - 11pt

	Water text Labels
		fill: 87cce9ff
		stroke: dbf1f9ff 1mm - stroke behind fill
		Paris: Arial Narrow - Bold Italic - 12pt
		Osaka: Hiragino Kaku Gothic Std - Normal - 10pt
		Seoul: Apple SD Gothic Neo - Ultra Bold - 10pt
